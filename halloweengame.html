Haunted Arena Lobby

Welcome to a local battle against bots. Choose a match mode first, then customize your character before deployment.

How to play: Move with WASD or arrow keys, aim with the mouse, hold left click to fire. Find weapon pickups, survive, and earn XP from eliminations to level up your fighter.

    Choose Game Mode

    Each match has 100 fighters total. In this local single-player version, that means you and 99 bots. Solo is every fighter for themselves. Teams balances the bots across two sides; your teammates will not target you.

    Solo and Teams matches start with 100 fighters (you plus 99 bots). The storm begins after 20 seconds, then closes over 2.5 minutes and damages fighters outside. Persistent mode has no storm and respawns eliminated bots to keep the arena populated.

    Character & Loadout

    Choose your starting weapon, costume, accessory, item, and drop location. Weapon pickups add guns to your wheel.

    Your first weapon. Find other weapon pickups during the match to add them to the Tab weapon wheel.

    Outfits have different models and combat abilities: Ghost moves faster; Pumpkin has extra health and regeneration; Skeleton deals more damage and takes less; Werewolf deals extra damage; Witch fires faster; Vampire heals on eliminations and pickups.

    Accessories provide real perks: Bat companion adds damage and regeneration; Floating lantern increases vision and damage; Graveyard chains add health; Hex charm improves firing; Wolf charm improves speed and damage.

    One-use item: Medkit heals 45; Ammo pouch gives ammo; Smoke bomb blocks sight; Dash tonic gives a short burst in your movement or aim direction; Haste potion boosts speed for 7 seconds. Speed charms in the arena boost movement for 10 seconds.

    Random drop spreads fighters across the map. Pillars mode starts fighters around special platforms with nearby gear.

    Cursed gear: optional choices at every 5 player eliminations (bots: every 10). Equip up to 3. Persistent mode keeps spawning new bots and can be used as an endless survival run.

    This version is a single-player local match against bots. It includes persistent survival mode, outfit abilities, and accessory boosts.

    In-match controls: move WASD / arrows · aim mouse · fire left click · reload R · use item 1 · press E beside a building to climb onto its roof or climb down · hold Tab and point to choose a weapon · hold right mouse with the Sniper Rifle for a wider view; keep your crosshair on an enemy to focus and zoom in for accuracy
